# Locality Mechanism Claims v1

## Allowed

- Removing edit gating causes strict-locality contract failures despite retaining nonzero edit response.
- With exact locality preserved, structural typed routing has higher Delta-IoU and better graph consistency than graphless routing.
- Dense routing leaks outside the declared region and has lower edit quality than typed routing.
- Typed routing is a mechanism ablation over rendered outputs; the full control-gated FloorFlow remains the quality-locality frontier.

## Forbidden

- Typed routing is the full FloorFlow method.
- Typed routing improves watertightness.
- A 0.99 outside average is acceptable under FEVP.
- Dense attention is a valid strict-locality substitute.
- The typed arm is a retrained cross-attention mechanism.
